ABOUT US

Located in the heart of Dublin, the National Hotel offers a warm welcome to travelers from around the world. Our hotel blends classic Irish charm with modern comfort, creating a relaxed environment for guests to unwind, explore, and enjoy everything the city has to offer. Whether you’re visiting for business, a weekend getaway, or a long-awaited vacation, our team is dedicated to making your stay as comfortable and memorable as possible. With spacious rooms, friendly service, and easy access to Dublin’s most iconic attractions, the National Hotel is the perfect base for your time in the city. From cozy breakfasts to recommendations on hidden local gems, we’re here to help you experience Dublin like a local. Your stay with us is more than just a room — it’s a chance to feel at home in one of Europe’s most vibrant cities.
